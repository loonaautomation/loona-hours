How It Works

Both clocks above are read live from the IANA time-zone database built into your browser — the same rule set your phone uses. “Difference today” is not a fixed fact about this pair of cities: it is computed from the two zones’ offsets right now, which is why it can legitimately read differently if you open this page in a different month.

Dubai is 8 hours ahead of Toronto for most of the year. For part of the year — when one city has changed its clocks and the other has not — the gap widens to 9 hours.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the time difference between Toronto and Dubai?

Dubai is 8 hours ahead of Toronto for most of the year. For part of the year — when one city has changed its clocks and the other has not — the gap widens to 9 hours.

When it is 9:00 AM in Toronto, what time is it in Dubai?

6:00 PM — that is 9:00 AM in Toronto converted on a January day, when Toronto is at UTC−5 and Dubai is at UTC+4. Check it any time with the converter above: the answer is worked out from the two zones’ offsets on the date you pick, so it stays right when the clocks change.

Does the Toronto–Dubai time difference change during the year?

Yes — the difference changes during the year, on the clock-change dates. Dubai is 8 hours ahead of Toronto for most of the year. For part of the year — when one city has changed its clocks and the other has not — the gap widens to 9 hours.

What is the best time for a call between Toronto and Dubai?

The two 9-to-5 working days do not overlap at all: 9:00 AM in Toronto is 5:00 PM in Dubai, and 5:00 PM in Toronto is 1:00 AM in Dubai. In practice, calls between Toronto and Dubai mean an early start on one side or a late finish on the other — use the converter above to find the least painful slot on the exact date.

How are the times on this page calculated?

From timestamps and the IANA time-zone database inside your browser — the live rules for each zone, including daylight-saving start and end dates and southern-hemisphere seasons where relevant. Nothing is hard-coded: open this page on a clock-change day and the clocks, the difference and the 24-hour table all follow the new offsets automatically.

How it works — the math

Two offsets and one subtraction — the same method the converter at the top of this page runs:

The formula:
Difference = Dubai’s UTC offset − Toronto’s UTC offset, both taken at the moment being converted
Dubai time = Toronto time + difference (crossing midnight moves the date, which the table marks +1 or −1 day)

Worked example

Take 9:00 AM in Toronto, on a January day, when Toronto is at UTC−5 and Dubai is at UTC+4. Adding the pair’s difference for that date gives 6:00 PM in Dubai. The subtlety is that the difference itself is date-dependent for this pair: repeat the same conversion in a month when one city has changed its clocks and the answer can move by an hour. That is why this page never prints a single fixed “the difference is X” without the word today next to it.
