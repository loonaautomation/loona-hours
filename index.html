Daily Overtime States

Five states add daily overtime on top of the federal weekly rule: California (daily 8/12 plus a seventh-day rule), Alaska (daily 8), Colorado (daily 12), Nevada (daily 8 for lower-wage workers) and Oregon (daily 10 in mills, factories and manufacturing). The Overtime Calculator by State applies each rule from a state selector and cites its official source; the California calculator goes deepest.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these calculators really free?

Yes — every tool on Loona Hours is free, with no sign-up, no account and no limits on calculations. The site is supported by advertising.

Is my timesheet data uploaded anywhere?

No. Calculations run in your browser, and the Save-my-week feature stores your week in your own browser's local storage on your device. Nothing you type is sent to us.

Which overtime rule should I use?

Start with the federal rule (overtime after 40 hours in a week) — it applies everywhere in the US. If you work in California, Alaska, Colorado, Nevada or Oregon, check the Overtime Calculator by State, because those states add daily overtime rules on top.

Why does my employer round my clock times?

Federal rules allow rounding punches to the nearest 5 minutes, tenth of an hour or quarter hour (the 7-minute rule), as long as it is neutral over time. The Time Rounding Calculator reproduces your employer's exact method so you can compare it with your real time.

Can I save my week and come back to it?

Yes — the Time Card Calculator autosaves the current week on your device and can archive finished weeks. Open the same browser on the same device and your week is exactly where you left it.
