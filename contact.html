What to Write About

  • A calculation looks wrong — send the page name, your inputs, and what you expected. Formula corrections with an official source are especially welcome and are acted on first.

  • A tool you wish existed — new calculators are chosen by real requests from hourly workers and small employers.

Please do not send personal payroll documents, Social Security numbers or pay stubs — a calculator question never needs them. We read every message; replies may take a few days, and there is no paid support tier or phone line.
